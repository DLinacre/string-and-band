    About this reference

    String & Band is an educational knowledge base on the history, materials, terminology and craft of archery equipment and slingshots. Content is provided for study, comparison and appreciation — it is not a set of construction instructions.

    Fully self-contained: no trackers, no external requests. Works offline once loaded.
